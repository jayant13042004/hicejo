## 2. Reddit Growth & Viral Distribution Strategy

> **Rule of Thumb:** Never post direct promotion links without providing massive genuine value first. Give away the free templates and advice, then mention Hicejo as the tool you built.

### Post 1: `r/resumes` & `r/jobs` (Value & Feedback Hook)
* **Title**: *I analyzed 500+ tech resumes that got interviews at Google, Stripe & Meta. Here are the 5 formatting mistakes that get 75% of people rejected (and how to fix them).*
* **Body Outline**:
  1. Share the 5 mistakes (Multi-column graphics, passive verbs, missing metrics, weird date formats, buzzword soup).
  2. Share the Google X-Y-Z formula (`Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z`).
  3. Offer: *"I built a free tool called Hicejo (hicejo.com) that calculates your ATS match score and roasts your resume with AI. No signup needed to test — let me know what you think!"*

### Post 2: `r/SideProject` & `r/webdev` (Builder Hook)
* **Title**: *I got tired of slow, paywalled resume builders, so I built an ultra-fast ATS resume optimizer with Next.js 16 and sub-second Gemini AI.*

  2. Highlight the 780ms sub-second generation benchmark.
  3. Ask for developer feedback on UI and performance.
