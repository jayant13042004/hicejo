# 💎 HICEJO — Official Brand Identity & Positioning Kit

---

## 1. The Brand Name

* **Primary Brand Name**: **Hicejo** (`hicejo.com`)
* **Pronunciation**: *Hi-seh-jo* or *Hi-see-jo*
* **Brand Meaning & Origin**:
  * **"Hi"** = Welcoming, Modern, Approachable.
  * **"Cejo" / "Ce"** = Career Acceleration & Executive Elevation.
  * **Why it works**: Short (6 letters), unique, memorable, passes the "radio test", and has a clean, premium tech feel like *Figma*, *Vercel*, or *Stripe*.

---

## 2. Mission & Vision Statements

### 🎯 The Mission Statement:
> *"To eliminate the friction between talent and opportunity by giving every job seeker sub-second, enterprise-grade AI tools to craft resumes that recruiters actually read."*

### 🔭 The Vision Statement:
> *"To build the world's most intelligent career copilot — empowering 10 million professionals to navigate modern hiring systems with total confidence, transparency, and speed."*

---

## 3. Brand Taglines & Value Propositions

* **Primary Tagline (Hero)**:
  > **"Build, tailor, and ATS-audit your resume in seconds."**
* **Short Brand Slogan**:
  > **"Your career, accelerated."**
* **Trust / Anti-Paywall Promise**:
  > **"No hostage paywalls. Just pure, sub-second career intelligence."**

---

## 4. Brand Archetype & Voice

* **The Archetype**: **The Empowering Strategist** (Competent, Direct, High-Speed, Encouraging, No Fluff).
* **Tone Attributes**:
  * **Ultra-Fast & Sharp**: Direct actionable advice, no robotic corporate jargon.
  * **Transparent & Honest**: We tell users the brutal truth about what ATS parsers actually care about.
  * **Empowering**: We treat the user like a top candidate who deserves their dream offer.

---

## 5. Visual Identity & Color Palette

* **Primary Accent**: Electric Indigo / Violet (`#6366f1` / `hsl(246 80% 60%)`) — *Signals high technology and AI intelligence.*
* **Success / ATS Accent**: Emerald Green (`#10b981`) — *Signals compliance, passing ATS screens, and interview success.*
* **Vibe / Roast Accent**: Flame Orange (`#f97316`) — *Signals energy, wit, and honest critique.*
* **Canvas Backgrounds**: Sleek Obsidian & Pure Paper White (`#09090b` / `#ffffff`).

---

## 6. Official Logo Design Specifications

* **The Icon**: A minimalist geometric **"H" Starburst / Spark** connecting two upward career pillars with a central focal spark.
* **The Wordmark**: Custom tracked sans-serif **Hice** in crisp stark white/black + **jo** in electric gradient violet.
